The First Two Weeks

How students choose band or chorus

4th and 5th graders try both before deciding. Two combined classes, then the groups split.

  1. Thursday, August 6

    Combined music class in the theater. Band and chorus together.

  2. Tuesday, August 11

    Combined again in the theater, and instrument assignments go out this day.

  3. Thursday, August 13

    Band and chorus meet separately for the first time. First day of band, in the band room. Every 4th and 5th grader comes in with their instrument and their book. Section seats are limited, so not every student gets their first choice.

Band is a full-year commitment. A section losing a player affects everyone in it, so students don't drop partway through except for a medical reason or special permission from administration. If band starts to feel impossible, email me before it gets to that point.

The Book

Provided, and yours to buy if you'd rather

4th and 5th grade use Essential Elements for Band, Book 1. A book is provided for every student, so there is nothing you have to purchase.

Buying your own is optional

Some families prefer their student have a copy that stays home and can be written in. If that's you, it's about $15 from Amazon or any music retailer. Get the edition for your student's specific instrument. A trumpet book won't work for a clarinet.

The One Thing That Matters Most

Practice 3 times a week, minimum 30 minutes

Class time alone isn't enough to make progress. Every band student practices at home, 3 times a week, 30 minutes minimum.

What counts as practice

Yes: your student playing on their own, at home.

No: band class on Tuesday and Thursday. That is rehearsal, not practice.

No: time in a private lesson. That is instruction, not practice.

Practicing after a lesson, working on what the teacher assigned, absolutely counts. Sitting in the lesson does not.

Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to rent a new instrument every year?
That depends on your family's arrangement with Joyful Noise. Talk to them directly about renewing or upgrading.
What if my student is behind on practice logs?
You'll get a direct email after 10+ days with nothing logged. It's a heads-up, not a penalty.
How do I reach Mr. Dillon-Seminario?
Email below. Every message gets a reply within 24 hours.
How is band graded?
4th and 5th grade band isn't a letter grade. It's an S / U / E mark. A student earns an S by putting in the effort and practicing, which is most of the class. An E is for going clearly beyond that. An S is a good mark, not a warning.
Where do I see grades and updates?
Veracross, updated every two weeks with what's happening in band.
