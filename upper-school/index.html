Upper School Band 2026-2027

Middle and high school. This is a performing ensemble, not a beginning class — here's what that means.

Starts Thursday, August 6

We play the first day

Upper School band starts Thursday, August 6 — the first week of school, and earlier than 4th and 5th grade band. Bring your instrument that day. We're not spending the first class handing things out; we're playing.

Come with your instrument chosen

Students should already know what they're playing and have their instrument in hand. Instrument selection isn't part of the first week the way it is in 4th grade.

Bring it August 6

If you rent through Joyful Noise, arrange it before then so the instrument is in your student's hands on day one.

Who This Class Is For

Experience required

Upper School band moves at the pace of a performing group. Students need enough playing behind them to keep up with the music, not just enthusiasm.

Middle School Band

At least 1–2 years of playing experience on the instrument. A student coming out of 5th grade band is well positioned.

High School Band

No new players. The music is too advanced to start from zero at this level. Students joining should already be playing.

Not sure whether your student is ready? Email me before the year starts rather than after. If middle school band isn't the right fit yet, there are better paths in — and it's a much easier conversation in July than in September.

Lessons

Not required — but this is where students separate

Private lessons aren't required for Upper School band. I'll be direct: the students carrying the strongest parts are almost always the ones taking them. At this level a teacher is the difference between holding a chair and leading a section.

Practice

3x a week, 30 minutes — same as every grade

The practice standard doesn't loosen as students get older. Minimum three times a week, 30 minutes a session, logged in MakeMusic Cloud. In a performing ensemble it matters more, not less — one unprepared part is audible.

How it's tracked

Practice is logged in MakeMusic Cloud. If 10+ days pass with nothing logged, you'll get a short, direct email — not a warning, just a nudge.

After-school rehearsals are required

In the weeks leading up to each concert there are required after-school rehearsals. This is the part families most often get caught out by, so plan around those windows — I'll send dates as far ahead as I can.

Grading

A letter grade

Unlike 4th and 5th grade band, Upper School band earns a letter grade. It reflects preparation, participation, and periodic playing assessments — scale quizzes from the Essential Elements book, or a short solo for percussionists. I email the details ahead of each assessment.
