Renting through Joyful Noise

The One Thing That Matters Most

Practice — 3x a week, 30 minutes, minimum

Every band and ukulele student practices at home at least 3 times a week, 30 minutes per session — every grade, no exceptions. This is what makes progress possible; class time alone isn't enough.

How it's tracked

Practice is logged in MakeMusic Cloud. If 10+ days pass with nothing logged, you'll get a short, direct email — not a warning, just a nudge.
