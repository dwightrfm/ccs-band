When we meet

Tuesday and Thursday

Band and ukulele both meet on the same two days every week. Instruments and books come to school on those days.

Class days

Tuesday Thursday

If your student forgets an instrument on a class day, that is a missed rehearsal, not a missed worksheet. There is no way to make it up at home.
